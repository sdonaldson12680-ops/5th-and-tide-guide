📺TV & Outdoor Movies⌄

Inside the camper

  1. Turn on the television.
  2. Turn on the soundbar manually, where applicable.
  3. Use the Fire Stick remote to navigate streaming apps and control the TV once everything is on.
  4. Sign into your own streaming services and sign out before checkout.

Outdoor projector

The ALVAR projector is set up with its own Fire Stick.

  1. Set the projector on a stable surface in its designated viewing area and connect power safely.
  2. Turn on the projector and select the Fire Stick HDMI input if needed.
  3. Hang the screen on the picket fence using the provided black S-hooks.
  4. If necessary, use the provided cord and tent stakes to secure the bottom; keep cords clear of walkways.
  5. Adjust the picture and enjoy your movie!
Bring the projector and electronics indoors after use. Take the screen down after movie night and never use it in strong wind or rain.

Projector, screen, hooks and stakes storage location: to be confirmed.

🔥Outdoor Living⌄

Enjoy the outdoor chairs, string lights, Solo Stove, charcoal grill and pellet smoker.

Fuel & supplies

A starter amount of charcoal and smoker pellets is provided in the labeled storage bin beneath the camper.

Lighter and charcoal-lighting supplies location: to be confirmed.

Firewood

Firewood bundles may be available for an additional fee by advance request. Message us through Airbnb to arrange.

Fire safety: Never use lighter fluid or other accelerants in the Solo Stove or pellet smoker. Use charcoal lighter only as directed for the charcoal grill. Keep fires and hot equipment away from the camper and fencing, supervise children, and never leave a fire unattended. Let equipment cool completely before handling ashes. Follow any applicable burn restrictions.

🚐RV Essentials⌄

Bathroom — important!

Only flush the provided RV-safe toilet paper. Never flush wipes (even “flushable” wipes), paper towels, hygiene products or anything else.

You'll find towels, shampoo, conditioner, body wash and basic first-aid essentials in the bathroom area.

Water & tanks

The camper is connected to utility hookups. Please don't adjust exterior tank valves or plumbing connections—we'll handle those. Contact us if anything seems wrong.

🦪Explore the Area⌄

5th & Tide is a great home base for waterfront dining, birdwatching, fishing and exploring Galveston Bay. We're not directly on a swimming beach.

  • Topwater Grill: Coastal dining and waterfront views.
  • Pier 6 Seafood & Oyster House: Seafood by the water.
  • Gilhooley's: A laid-back local oyster favorite.
  • Mooner's Beach: A small shoreline spot for sitting and watching boats—not a traditional swimming beach.
  • Kemah Boardwalk: Restaurants, rides and waterfront entertainment.
  • Galveston: Beaches, the Strand and sightseeing.

  • Lock the camper door and follow the departure gate instructions.
