Sleeping Arrangements

5th & Tide sleeps up to six guests, with a king-size bedroom and two sofas that convert into queen beds.

Fresh linens, pillows, blankets, towels, and washcloths are provided.

Our TVs use Amazon Fire Sticks. Turn on the soundbar manually where applicable, then use the Fire Stick remote.

🌙 Outdoor Living & Movie Nights

Outdoor Movie Night

Our ALVAR projector has its own Fire Stick.

Hang the projector screen on the fence using the provided black hooks. The bottom can be secured with the supplied cord and tent stakes if needed.

Please bring electronics inside after use and take down the screen when finished, especially if it's windy or raining.

Fire Pit

Enjoy relaxing by the Solo Stove and outdoor seating. Firewood may be available upon request for an additional fee.

Grilling & Smoking

We provide a charcoal grill and pellet smoker.

A starter supply of charcoal and pellets is available in the labeled storage bin underneath the camper.

Please use each grill according to its instructions. Never use lighter fluid in the Solo Stove or pellet smoker.

Keep flames supervised and away from the camper and fencing, and observe any local burn restrictions.

🚿 Bathroom & Essentials

We've stocked the bathroom with towels, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, and basic first-aid essentials.

A small favor from our plumbing: Please flush ONLY the RV-safe toilet paper provided. All wipes, hygiene products, paper towels, and other items belong in the trash.

The camper is connected to utilities, so there's no need to handle any exterior tanks or valves. We've taken care of those for you!

🦪 Explore the Area

5th & Tide is a great home base for enjoying Galveston Bay and the surrounding coastal communities.

  • Topwater Grill: Waterfront dining
  • Pier 6: Seafood and waterfront views
  • Gilhooley's: A laid-back local favorite
  • Mooner's Beach: A small shoreline for relaxing and watching the boats, rather than swimming
  • Kemah Boardwalk: Dining and attractions
  • Galveston: Beaches, shopping, the Strand, and sightseeing

We're not directly on the beach, but there are plenty of opportunities nearby to enjoy the coast, watch shorebirds, and explore.

🧳 Easy Checkout

Checkout is at 11:00 AM unless otherwise arranged.

No deep cleaning needed! Before you leave, we just ask that you:

  • Wash used dishes (the drying rack is fine)
  • Throw away leftover food
  • Gather trash for disposal
  • Place used towels in the shower
  • Gather used sofa bedding together
  • Sign out of personal streaming accounts
  • Turn off lights and electronics
  • Put away games and outdoor movie equipment
  • Secure the camper door when leaving

Thank you for helping us keep 5th & Tide welcoming for our next guests!
